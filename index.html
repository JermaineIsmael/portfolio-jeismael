05 / Systems thinking

Architecture & system design

Simplified examples of how I think about dependable, observable systems.

Cloud-native application

Design for change, failure, and clear ownership.

01 / Inbound validation

External systems send structured payloads into normalized inbound adapters where format and contract checks happen before the event is routed.

02 / Routing intelligence

A central router applies deterministic rules to classify, transform, and fan out each payload to the correct downstream system or partner.

03 / Controlled delivery

Outbound adapters serialize the message format, handle retries, and maintain partner-specific behavior without coupling the core platform to every external API.

04 / Observability

Logs, tracing, alerting, and metrics make failures visible early and help teams keep the platform dependable in production.

Architecture examples are simplified representations intended to demonstrate engineering concepts and do not disclose confidential client information.
